About Me

My name is Anas Moukalalah. I am a second-year PhD student at Cadi Ayyad University in Morocco.

My academic research focuses on Artificial Intelligence, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs) and their ability to interact with external tools.

My PhD Research

Research Topic

Exploring tool selection methods for Large Language Models, with a focus on approaches that do not require few-shot examples in the deployed prompt.

Current Progress

I am currently working on a systematic literature review to understand existing approaches, challenges, and opportunities in this research area.
